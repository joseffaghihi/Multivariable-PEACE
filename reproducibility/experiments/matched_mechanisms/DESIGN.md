# Matched-summary causal mechanisms: what information does the PEACE surface add?

Design and exact identities fixed before simulation. This is a constructed separation example, not evidence of universal superiority or an exclusive novelty claim. It directly addresses comparisons with average effects, distribution-aware/information causal measures and derivative sensitivity. Previous experiments and the open estimation document remain untouched.

Two standardized controller settings T in [0,1] have the same reference density across three equal-width intervals: rho=(.5,1,1.5), hence probabilities (1/6,1/3,1/2). An observed pre-treatment operating profile Z is common with probability .9 and rare with probability .1. T and Z are independent by randomized assignment. Y=h_M(T,Z)+epsilon, epsilon independent Normal(0,.02^2). There is no confounding. The causal response is h_M(t,z)=E[Y_do(T=t)|Z=z]. With u=3t-j inside treatment interval j, h=sin^2(pi*k_jz*u). The response and its first derivative join continuously at interval boundaries, all returning to zero. No boundary atoms exist. Supports/units and output amplitudes are identical.

Define a=(4,1,2), b=(3,3,1). Mechanism A uses b in common profiles and 4a=(16,4,8) in rare profiles. Mechanism B uses a in common profiles and 4b=(12,12,4) in rare profiles. All local changes are genuine interventions. Context remains explicit; r uses powers of categorical profile probabilities with counting measure. The task is to characterize how cumulative local causal movement is distributed over settings and profiles, not to select a beneficial treatment or maximize outcome. Larger magnitude means more movement, not a preferable mechanism.

Exact matching: sum a=sum b=7, rho·a=rho·b=6, rho·a^2=rho·b^2=15. Thus A and B have equal endpoint contrasts (zero at all interval boundaries), signed average derivatives (zero), uniform treatment total variation for each profile, natural E|h'| for each profile, and natural E(h'^2) for each profile. Under uniform draws within any interval, sin^2(pi*k*u) has the same Beta(1/2,1/2) output law for every positive integer k. Hence entire outcome distributions, every marginal outcome quantile/moment and signal variance are identical, even under piecewise-constant escort treatment weights and reweighted profiles. Gaussian noise preserves equality and makes information measures finite.

Because T and Z are independent parents, Janzing et al.'s edge strength for T→Y reduces to I(T;Y|Z) (Theorem 4). This is H(Beta(1/2,1/2)+Gaussian noise)−H(Gaussian noise), identical for both mechanisms. This does not assert equality of every causal edge measure in their DAGs or equality of full conditional response curves. Quantile treatment effects for the declared boundary contrasts are zero; interior contrasts can distinguish the mechanisms.

Even Sobol signal decompositions match: distinct common/rare integer frequencies within every interval make their centered sine-square responses orthogonal. Var(h)=1/8, Var(E[h|T])=(.9^2+.1^2)/8=.1025, Var(E[h|Z])=0, and interaction variance=.0225, for both mechanisms. Noise variance=.0004. Normalized first/total indices for T/Z and explained-variance measures therefore agree. These equalities are constructed controls, not claims that such measures are invalid.

PEACE normalized conditional response score:
 P_M(d,r)=sum_z pi_z^r/sum(pi^r) × [sum_j rho_j^(2d)*2*k_M,jz]/[(1/3)*sum_j rho_j^(2d)].
Let Delta=(1,-2,1). Then
 P_A−P_B=2[.5^(2d)−2+1.5^(2d)]/B_d × [4*.1^r−.9^r]/[.9^r+.1^r].
Equality holds at d=0 and d=.5 for every r, and at r*=log(4)/log(9) for every d. At d=1,r=1, A=96/7 and B=99/7; at d=1,r=0, A=192/7 and B=183/7. Thus a full surface distinguishes where movement lies, despite matching the listed scalars, and the ranking changes with an explicitly declared profile emphasis. Predeclare degree grid d in [0,.25,.5,1,2], r in [0,.5,1,2] and dense surfaces d∈[0,2],r∈[0,2]. No test-driven degree optimization.

Include a strengthened comparator: density/profile-weighted squared-derivative measure on the exact same escort law. It can also distinguish mechanisms, but measures derivative energy rather than accumulated absolute movement and need not have PEACE's ranking or profile crossover. An arbitrarily weighted absolute-derivative measure with identical weights equals PEACE by construction; do not claim exclusive separation from that identical functional.

Operational check: average magnitudes of small controlled local mean-response contrasts, divided by step size, under the declared escort law approach PEACE as the step decreases. Use centered steps delta=[1/50,1/200,1/800,1/3200], restricting/renormalizing to admissible centers. Coarse steps can alias oscillations; the infinitesimal metric is not automatically the appropriate answer for large indivisible interventions. This check is separate from the noisy-data estimator and verifies interpretation, not a new prediction claim.

Finite-data recovery: N=[2000,8000,32000], 40 paired noisy calibration datasets per N. Calibration sampling is profile-stratified, half common/half rare, with T sampled from its natural reference density in each profile. This deliberate oversampling is not the natural profile population. Reference profile probabilities and treatment bin probabilities are estimated from a separate 20,000-point unlabeled natural reference sample, shared by both mechanisms. Conditional independence of T,Z is supplied by randomization; no hidden-confounding robustness claim.

Fit unknown responses in a declared bounded spectral family: per profile and treatment interval, regress outcomes on sin^2(pi*m*u), m=1,...,20. Exact generating frequencies and coefficients are NOT supplied; they lie within this predeclared candidate family. The family imposes return-to-baseline and zero slopes at interval boundaries; boundary zeros in fitted contrasts are restrictions, not discovered causal nulls. Ordinary least squares fits all coefficients; no oracle feature selection. This is model-conditional estimation, not unrestricted nonparametric identification or a generic learner rate. Compute absolute variation using extrema of the fitted trigonometric polynomial and powered-density normalizers, and squared derivatives by orthogonality. Report full estimates, bias, RMSE, paired differences, decision/ranking frequencies and Monte Carlo SE. Retain all sample sizes, degrees and adverse estimates. All baseline comparisons use the same fitted responses, with analytic oracle matching as the primary argument.

Validate symbolic matching identities, known score fractions/crossovers, equality of output-mixture densities and finite information measures, independent derivative/root integration, density normalizers, finite-step convergence and complete paired replicate counts. Interpret degree curves with the manuscript's log-density covariance identity and the corresponding profile-weight identity, not by visually labelling any decreasing curve a negative effect.
